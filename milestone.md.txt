## Important Decisions

- NjeKlik remains an admin-controlled auction platform, not a marketplace.
- Only admins can create products and auctions.
- Users must be logged in and have complete delivery details before bidding.
- Supabase is the source of truth for live data; local memory data remains a fallback for sandbox/dev behavior.
- Temporary production data is approved for release validation and must be clearly named and cleaned up where safe.
- Uploaded logo files are the source for transparent NjeKlik logo assets.
